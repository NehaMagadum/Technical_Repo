## Question 13 - difference between shallow copy and deep copy
### Answer -

## Question 17 - Explain ACID property in sql
### Answer - 
- A – Atomicity: A transaction happens completely or not at all.
- C – Consistency: A transaction maintains database rules and integrity.
- I – Isolation: Transactions execute independently without interfering.
- D – Durability: Committed changes remain permanently saved.
